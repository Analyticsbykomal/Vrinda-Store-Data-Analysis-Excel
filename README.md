# Vrinda-Store-Data-Analysis–Excel-Dashboard

# 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Vrinda Store's sales data for the year 2022 using Microsoft Excel.

The main objective of this project is to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, order trends, and sales channel performance through an interactive Excel dashboard.

# 2. Business Questions

The project aims to answer the following business questions:

1. Compare sales and orders using a single chart.
2. Which month recorded the highest sales and orders?
3. Who purchased more – men or women in 2022?
4. What are the different order statuses in 2022?
5. Which are the top 10 states contributing to sales?
6. What is the relationship between age and gender based on the number of orders?
7. Which sales channel contributes the most?
8. Which is the highest-selling category?

# 3. Tools & Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Interactive Dashboard

# 4. Key Business Insights

- Women contribute approximately 65% of total purchases compared to men.
- Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states contributing to sales.
- The adult age group (30–49 years) contributes approximately 50% of total orders.
- Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the top contributing sales channels, accounting for approximately 80% of sales.

# 5. Business Recommendations

- Target women customers aged 30–49 years, especially those living in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
- Promote products through attractive advertisements, special offers, and discount coupons.
- Focus on major sales channels such as Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra to increase customer engagement and boost sales.

# 6. Project Files

- **Excel Dashboard:** Interactive sales dashboard.
- **Original Dataset:** Vrinda Store sales data for 2022.
- **Project Questions:** Business questions used for analysis.
- **Business Insights:** Key findings and recommendations.

# 7. Project Goal

To analyze sales data, identify customer purchasing patterns, understand business performance, and generate meaningful insights that can help Vrinda Store make better business decisions.
